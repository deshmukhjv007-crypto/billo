## Full description (4000 max)

```
Stop splitting bills. Just send them. 🧾

Group trips shouldn't end in awkward math. Billo is the bill splitter that doesn't ask you to bookkeep — it asks you to send the bill.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Start a trip (Goa Trip, Manali Trip, office offsite) and add your people.
2. Send the bills:
   • Snap it — photograph a restaurant bill, hotel invoice, cab receipt, UPI screenshot.
   • Say it — type "Paid ₹800 cab for me and Rahul" and Billo parses it.
   • Quick — two taps for a cash expense.
3. Confirm. Billo keeps the ledger, learns your group's patterns, and shows exactly who pays whom.

THE KILLER PART
No categories to pick. No amounts to retype. No "who was in this?" — Billo suggests, you tap, done. Photo → Confirm → Done.

WHEN THE TRIP ENDS
• One-tap "settle up" — the fewest possible payments (Rahul → Jay ₹1,820).
• A beautiful shareable wrap-up card for the group WhatsApp:
  🏔️ Manali Trip — ₹47,280 · 5 people · 37 bills · ₹9,456/person
• Backups you own: export/import your data anytime.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. If you turn on AI bill-reading, photos go only to the API you configure — never to us.

BILLO PRO (optional)
Receipt archive, 12 currencies, any AI endpoint, and clean share cards. Free otherwise — all the splitting is free, forever.

Billo — just send the bills. We'll do the math.
```
