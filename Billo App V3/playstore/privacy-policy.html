Billo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026
Effective date: 25 September 2026

Short version: Billo is an on-device bill-splitting app. By default your trips, bills and balances live only on your phone. If you choose to share a trip live with friends, that trip's names, bills, payments and UPI IDs are stored on our server (Google Firebase) so everyone in the trip sees the same thing. Receipt photos never leave your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. What the app does

Billo helps groups split expenses for trips, events and shared living. You create trips, add expenses (manually, from a text description, or from a photo of a bill), see who owes whom, and share a summary card. Everything happens on your device unless you turn on live sync for a trip (section 3).

2. Data we collect

For trips you keep on your phone: none. Billo has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no crash reporting. Trips you don't share live never leave your device.

3. Live sync (optional, per trip)

When you tap Turn on live sync on a trip, or join a trip with a code, Billo stores that trip on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, operated by Google on our behalf) so everyone in the trip can add bills from their own phone. For a shared trip we store:

  • the trip's name, emoji, currency, budget and join code;
  • the names people in the trip are listed under, and the UPI IDs they choose to add;
  • the bills (amount, description, category, date, who paid, who shared it, item lines, and the text you typed to add it) and the settle-up payments recorded;
  • a random anonymous ID for each phone in the trip (Firebase Anonymous Authentication) — no name, email, phone number or Google account is attached to it.

Never uploaded: receipt photos, your AI API key, trips you haven't shared, and the app's settings.

Who can see it: only phones that have joined that trip. Joining requires the trip's code, so share it only with people in the trip. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google. We don't sell it, share it or use it for anything other than showing the trip to its members.

Leaving and deleting: "Leave trip" removes the trip from your phone and stops your phone's access; the other members keep their copy. To have a shared trip deleted from our server, email us from section 11 with the trip code — we delete it within 30 days. Server data for trips nobody has opened in 24 months may be deleted.

4. Bill photo reading

By default, bill photos are processed entirely on your device. Billo includes an on-device OCR engine; no photo is ever sent to anyone, and this works without an account or any API key.

Optionally, you can enable cloud AI reading in Settings and enter your own API key (e.g. OpenAI or any OpenAI-compatible provider). Only in that case, and only for the duration of a read:

  • The photo (compressed) and a short extraction prompt are sent directly from your device to the API endpoint you configure.
  • We are not the recipient of that data; the provider's privacy policy (e.g. OpenAI's) governs it. The photo is used only to extract the expense fields and is not stored by Billo.
  • Your API key is stored only in your device's local storage and sent only to the endpoint you configured.
  • You can turn the feature off at any time. Without it, no photo ever leaves your device (free-plan photos are processed and discarded; Pro stores them in your local receipt archive).

5. Google Play Billing

If you purchase Billo Pro, the transaction is processed by Google Play. Google receives standard purchase data (your Google account, product, amount, receipt) under its own privacy policy. The app stores a local "pro_unlocked" flag and can restore it from Google Play's purchase history on launch.

6. Permissions

  • Internet — used for live sync of trips you choose to share, the optional AI bill-reading endpoint you configure, and Google Play Billing. Nothing else is transmitted.
  • Camera / media — only when you choose to add a bill photo. There is no background access.

7. Children's privacy

Billo is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country) and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

8. Data deletion

Delete everything on your phone anytime in-app (Settings → Erase everything) or by uninstalling the app. For shared trips stored on our server, use "Leave trip" and email us to delete the trip itself (section 3).

9. Sharing / selling data

Never. Shared-trip data is processed by Google Firebase as our service provider, only to run live sync.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what we store or who can see it, we will update this page, its date, and the Play Store data-safety section before the change reaches you.
