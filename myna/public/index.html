Usage policy

What Myna will and will not do.

  • Built for mock interviews, prep, drills, transcription you are allowed to make, and after-the-fact review.
  • No anti-detection, no screen-share hiding, no proctoring evasion. None is planned.
  • Never use it where the rules forbid assistance — proctored tests, take-home assessments, or an employer that bans AI. Getting caught costs the offer and the reference.
  • Recording a call you are on is your responsibility: consent and wiretap law vary by country and state.
  • Your resume, transcript and key never leave your machine except to the endpoint you configure.
